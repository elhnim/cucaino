💡 What This Game Just Taught Us

Mia took a big income hit in Year 2 by switching to a harder gig — but by Year 3 she was earning $15,000/yr net vs Alex's $2,200. That one brave move compounded for 3 years. Alex played it safe, maxed out investments early, and will carry the best passive income foundation into Phase 2. Neither path is wrong — both lead to freedom. The lesson: your decisions today shape your options tomorrow.
