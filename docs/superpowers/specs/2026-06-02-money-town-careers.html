🎲 Overview & Concept

Live a whole life in an hour — earn, learn, invest, and out-strategize your friends to financial freedom first.

Each turn = one year of your life. Players race from their teens to financial freedom — the moment your money earns enough to cover your living costs, so you never have to work again. First one free wins. The whole design proves there are many roads to freedom: the high earner, the savvy investor, the risk-taking founder, and the frugal saver can all win — so no single strategy dominates.

The journey — 3 phases (age 13 → freedom)

1Teen Gigs4 turns (13→17)random gig; earn, save, build skills; earn your career gate
2Education / Trainingcareer-specificinvest in yourself; learn good vs bad debt
3Established Careeruntil freedom / age 65the main game — build a passive-income engine
The two ways to win (the heart): Freedom = passive income ≥ expenses → 📈 grow income (buy assets that pay you) or 📉 shrink expenses (live modestly, own your home, kill debt). A flashy doctor and a frugal tortoise can both cross the line.

🏁 The Spine — win condition & luck

🏆 Win: first player whose passive income ≥ expenses wins (FIRE). Passive income = net cash yield of all assets (rent + dividends + interest + profit − asset expenses). Backstop: highest net worth at age 65.

Two levers (un-dominable)

📈 Grow income — income assets (commercial, business, paid-off property).
📉 Shrink expenses — live modestly, own your home, kill debt interest.

4% rule is taught as a printed rule-of-thumb ("aim for ~25× expenses"), but the win math is real per-asset income — so a growth-only pile (crypto) doesn't win until harvested.

The luck dial (~25%)

≤ 2 random checks/year, capped magnitude, and buy-down (insurance halves bad events; diversification halves variance).

Skilled play pulls realised variance below 25%; reckless play (all crypto, no insurance) pushes it above — their choice, not the baseline.

2️⃣ Phase 2 — Education / Training framework

Invest years in yourself for a bigger Phase 3, and learn good vs bad debt. Each career has its own timeline — the contrast is the lesson.

Turn loop & the shared training actions

Same Act → Reel → Settle rhythm. Each training year, pick one: TA-MASTER Pro Skills +1 (career rank → graduating salary) · TA-INVEST Money Smarts +1 + deploy cash · TA-OVERTIME +flat cash, no skill (earn now) · TA-TOOLUP good debt → income boost.

Career timelines

ArchetypeLengthWhile training
Earn-while-learn (Tradie, Farmer)3 yrlow rising pay, little debt
Study-heavy (Doctor, Lawyer, Banker)5–6 yr$0 + high debt
Mixed (Teacher, Tycoon)2–4 yrpart-time pay
Talent (Athlete, Musician, Artist, Influencer)0–2 yrboom/bust

Debt = the mirror of investing

An investment grows your money; a debt grows what you owe — same compounding, opposite direction. Interest is a recurring expense (pushes the win bar up). Repay anytime.

🟢 Good debt — borrow to buy something that earns (tools, a mortgage).
🔴 Bad debt — borrow for things that lose value ("V8 on finance?").

3️⃣ Phase 3 — Established Career the main game

Turn = 1 year, graduation → freedom (or 65). A strong player frees themselves in their 30s–40s.

The yearly loop — plan, then live the year

1. Act (your plan, resolved first) → 2. Reel (life lands on the position you took) → 3. Settle (income + passive, pay expenses, assets grow/shrink + events, debts grow) → 4. Check win. Insurance bought this turn protects this turn; crypto bought is exposed immediately.

The one action (≈75% decision)

ActionEffect
🏠 Buy an assetnow in play + exposed to its deck
📈 Work Harder (rank ↑)higher salary + auto lifestyle
🏦 Pay down debtcuts this year's interest
🛡️ Buy insurancesoftens a bad reel this year
🏡 Buy home outrightremoves housing slice (lever)
🪙 Downsizelifestyle a bracket below rank (lever)
💱 Harvest / Convertsell growth asset → income / debt payoff

Lifestyle inflation — auto & sub-linear

Each rank carries a default lifestyle cost — but a smaller % of salary as you climb (Tradie 68%→65%→61%→57%). Higher ranks get a better savings rate, so climbing stays competitive with frugality.

Expense levers (active): 🪙 Downsize · 🏡 own your home · 🏦 kill debt · 🧗 don't over-climb. This is what makes the 🐢 Frugal Tortoise viable.

Win paths — all viable

🏢 Entrepreneurbusiness owner, huge passive incomehigh

Cross-player layer

🔨 Asset auctions — bid against rivals · 🌍 market events hit everyone · 🃏 social cards help/hinder · 🏆 visible race → catch-up tension to the last turn.

How to read this · coding scheme

Each career has a 3-letter prefix. Every item is coded so we can reference it precisely during balance + build.

  • XXX-K1…K4 — the 4 career ranks (salary tiers). Mapped onto the shared Pro Skills ladder: K1=entry (PS-L01–03) · K2=PS-L04 · K3=PS-L07 · K4=PS-L09.
  • XXX-O01… — Opportunities (positive career events).
  • XXX-R01… — Risks (negative career events).
  • Phase 3 reel = universal life deck + the asset decks (§6) + this career's O/R decks.
  • Lifestyle column = the auto sub-linear living cost baked into each rank (a smaller % of salary as you climb).

Shared Phase 2 training actions (skinned per career): TA-MASTER Pro Skills +1 (rank) · TA-INVEST Money Smarts +1 + deploy cash · TA-OVERTIME +flat cash, no skill · TA-TOOLUP good debt → income boost. Career length = number of training turns.
