How it works:
· Three sections per day: Before school (school activities) · Class periods (subjects) · After school (school activities)
· The subject registry in subject-registry.ts covers both class subjects and school activities
· Subjects and school activities flow into Todo as read-only display rows — they are not tasks and earn no stars
· External activities (Karate, Piano, etc.) are tasks added to the After school section of Todo separately
· "✏️ Timetable" button in Todo opens the full grid; also accessible from Avatar → Edit profile → My Timetable
· Overriding a single day from Todo only affects that one day — template unchanged for future weeks
