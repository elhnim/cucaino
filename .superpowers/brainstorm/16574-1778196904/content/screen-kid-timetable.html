Design decisions:
· Timetable accessed via a dedicated screen (not inline in Todo) — keeps Todo clean
· Entry point: "✏️ Timetable" button on the School section header in Todo
· Grid scrolls horizontally on phone — all 5 days always present
· Subject picker shows standard subjects + kid can later have custom ones
· "None / Clear slot" option for free periods
· "+ Add period row" to support schools with 5 or 6 periods
· Saved template applies from next day onward — current day uses whatever was set
