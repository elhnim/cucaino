Rules:
· Parent sets reason, penalty amount (or warning-only), and when deduction happens (now / after 24h / manual)
· Kid sees the strike prominently on their Home screen with countdown if deferred
· Strikes don't affect Level — only the star balance
· Parent can clear a strike at any time (e.g. kid makes amends) — no deduction occurs
· Multiple strikes stack — each shown separately
