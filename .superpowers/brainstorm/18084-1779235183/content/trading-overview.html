📝 Design notes
  • Nuggets (🪙) are the in-game currency — 1 star = 1,000 Nuggets
  • Portfolio tab shows balance, P/L summary, and all holdings
  • Market tab shows the 10 companies in a 2-column grid — green badge for owned assets
  • Asset detail sheet slides up — shows 30-day chart, today's news, buy/sell controls
  • Rankings tab shows family leaderboard sorted by % gain (fair for all deposit sizes)
  • Dividend notifications appear as a toast/banner in the portfolio tab
