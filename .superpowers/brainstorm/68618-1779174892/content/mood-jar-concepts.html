A — Bubble Jar

Each mood tap drops an emoji bubble into the jar. The jar fills up throughout the day. Playful and literal — you can see the mix of moods at a glance.

Each mood has a colour. The jar fills in coloured layers proportional to how often each mood was logged. Shows the balance of the day at a glance.
