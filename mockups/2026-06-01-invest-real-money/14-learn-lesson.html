What is a stock? 🏢

🍕

A company is like a pizza

Imagine a company is a big pizza. A stock (also called a share) is one slice of that pizza. When you buy a share, you own a tiny piece of the whole company! 🍕

If lots of people want slices of that company — because it's doing really well — the slices become worth more. If the company struggles, the slices can become worth less.

💡 Key idea

Owning a share means you own a small piece of a real company — and you share in its ups and downs.

Real example

🎮

If you buy one share of Roblox, you own a tiny slice of the whole Roblox company. When more kids play Roblox, the company can be worth more — and so is your slice.

Quick check ✏️

What do you get when you buy a share?

A free toy from the company
A tiny piece of the company 🍕
A coupon for the shops
